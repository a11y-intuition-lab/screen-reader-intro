Get started with a mobile screen reader

This is an introduction for sighted people who design, build or test digital services. You learn to use the screen reader on your own phone: VoiceOver on iPhone or TalkBack on Android.

Your screen reader reads the introduction to you. You practise while you hear how it works.

Choose your phone

  • iPhone: VoiceOver
  • Android: TalkBack

Before you start

The first part is five short steps and takes about 15 minutes. Headphones help.

You can add the introduction to your home screen, so it opens like an app. On iPhone, tap Share in Safari and choose Add to Home Screen. On Android, open the Chrome menu and choose Add to Home screen.

This is not a simulation of being blind. People who use a screen reader every day have spent years getting good at it, and most of them listen far faster than you will today. The goal is to understand the tool well enough to test what you make, and to hear the difference between good and bad code.

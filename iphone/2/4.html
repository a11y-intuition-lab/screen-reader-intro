Part 2, step 4 of 5 Form fields

In a form, VoiceOver reads the name of the field, what kind of field it is, and any hint that belongs to it.

You can jump between form fields with the rotor. Rotate two fingers on the screen as if you were turning a dial. VoiceOver says the name of each setting. Keep turning until you hear “Form Controls”. Then swipe down to go to the next one, and up to go to the previous one.

Double-tap a text field to bring up the keyboard. Typing with a screen reader takes time, so type just a few letters. Find a letter with your finger and lift your finger, or double-tap, to type it.

While the keyboard is open, you can move to the next field with the rotor: choose Form Controls and swipe down.

Try it: first press “Sign up” without filling anything in. Listen to what happens. Then fill in the form and sign up.

When something is wrong, the page moves you to the field, and VoiceOver reads the field name together with the error message. If the error were only shown in red, you would not have heard it.

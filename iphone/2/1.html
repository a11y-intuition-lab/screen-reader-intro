Part 2, step 1 of 5 Headings

Most screen reader users don't read a page from the top. They jump between headings to get an overview, just as you skim with your eyes. Two out of three start this way on a long page.

You jump between headings with the rotor. Rotate two fingers on the screen as if you were turning a dial. VoiceOver says the name of each setting. Keep turning until you hear “Headings”. Then swipe down to go to the next one, and up to go to the previous one.

If you never hear “Headings”, add it: open Settings, then Accessibility, then VoiceOver, then Rotor, and turn on Headings.

VoiceOver also says the heading level, such as “heading level 3”. The levels tell you how the page is organised.

Try it: find out when Fjordby Library closes on Saturday. Use headings to jump there.

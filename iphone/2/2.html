Part 2, step 2 of 5 Landmarks

Landmarks are the main areas of a page: the header at the top, navigation menus, the main content and the footer. Some screen reader users jump between them to skip things that repeat on every page, such as menus.

Landmarks are in the rotor too. Rotate two fingers on the screen as if you were turning a dial. VoiceOver says the name of each setting. Keep turning until you hear “Landmarks”. Then swipe down to go to the next one, and up to go to the previous one.

If you never hear “Landmarks”, add it: open Settings, then Accessibility, then VoiceOver, then Rotor, and turn on Landmarks.

Try it: this page has a header, the main content with a practice area, the menu at the bottom called “Steps”, and a footer. Jump to the menu called “Steps”, then back to the main content.

Each landmark needs a name when there is more than one of the same kind. That is why the menu at the bottom is called “Steps”, so you know what kind of navigation it is.

Practice area: Fjordby Library

This practice area is a landmark of its own, because it has a name. Did you hear it when you jumped between landmarks?

Swiping left and right still moves one thing at a time, whatever the rotor is set to. Swipe right until you hear “Next”, and double-tap.

Step 1 of 5 Turn it off before you turn it on

Read this step before you turn on VoiceOver.

When VoiceOver is on, your phone works differently. One tap selects something and reads it aloud. You tap twice to open or activate what you selected. That is why you set up a shortcut first.

  1. Open Settings, then Accessibility, then Accessibility Shortcut.
  2. Choose VoiceOver.
  3. Come back to this page.

You can now turn VoiceOver on and off by pressing the side button three times quickly. If your phone has a Home button, press that three times instead.

Stuck? Press the side button three times. Or tell Siri: “Turn off VoiceOver”.

When the shortcut is ready, tap Next at the bottom of the screen.

Step 1 of 5 Turn it off before you turn it on

Read this step before you turn on TalkBack. If you can't find an option, search for “TalkBack” in Settings.

When TalkBack is on, your phone works differently. One tap selects something and reads it aloud. You tap twice to open or activate what you selected. That is why you set up a shortcut first.

  1. Open Settings, then Accessibility, then TalkBack.
  2. Turn on the TalkBack shortcut.
  3. Come back to this page.

You can now turn TalkBack on and off by holding both volume keys for three seconds. The first time, your phone asks whether you want to use the shortcut.

Stuck? Hold both volume keys for three seconds. Or tell Google Assistant: “Turn off TalkBack”.

When the shortcut is ready, tap Next at the bottom of the screen.

Step 2 of 5 Turn on TalkBack

Hold both volume keys for three seconds. You hear a voice, and a coloured frame appears around whatever TalkBack has selected.

From now on TalkBack reads this page to you. To move on, swipe right with one finger, anywhere on the screen. Try it now.

Good. Each swipe right moves to the next thing on the page. Swipe left to go back.

Tap once with three fingers. If a menu opens, your phone supports multi-finger gestures, which this introduction uses. Close the menu by swiping down then left in one movement. If no menu opens, your phone uses one-finger gestures only. You will find them in the TalkBack tutorial, in TalkBack settings.

At the bottom of the screen there is a menu with Back, Menu and Next, like the tab bar in an app. Keep swiping right until you hear “Next”. Then double-tap anywhere on the screen.

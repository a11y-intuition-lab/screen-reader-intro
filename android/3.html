Step 3 of 5 Listen and stop

You will hear far more than you need. The most important skill is stopping the speech.

Tap once with two fingers to stop. Tap with two fingers again to continue.

Practise on this paragraph. It is long on purpose. Screen reader users skim by hearing the first few words and moving on if it is not what they are looking for. That is why the most important words should come first in a text, a link and a button. When you hear that a paragraph is not what you need, just swipe on. You don't have to wait until it has been read to the end. Try stopping the speech now, with two fingers.

To read the whole page from the top, open the TalkBack menu by tapping with three fingers, and choose “Read from top”. Stop with two fingers whenever you like.

TalkBack scrolls the page as you swipe. To scroll yourself, swipe up or down with two fingers.

Swipe right until you hear “Next”, and double-tap.

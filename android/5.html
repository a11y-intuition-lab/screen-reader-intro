Step 5 of 5 Hide screen

TalkBack can turn the screen black while TalkBack keeps working. You then have to rely on what you hear, as many screen reader users do.

Open the TalkBack menu by tapping with three fingers, and choose “Hide screen”. Choose the same option in the menu to show the screen again.

Try it: hide the screen. Swipe through this page, then find Next at the bottom right with your finger and double-tap. You can leave the screen hidden.

Can't get the screen back? Open the TalkBack menu with three fingers and choose to show the screen. If that doesn't help, turn off TalkBack by holding both volume keys.

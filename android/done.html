You can now turn TalkBack on and off, move back and forth, stop the speech, find things with your finger, activate them and hide the screen.

If the screen is still hidden, open the TalkBack menu with three fingers and choose to show it.

Next up is part 2: headings, landmarks, links and buttons, forms and status messages.

For more practice, TalkBack has its own tutorial. Open the TalkBack menu with three fingers, choose TalkBack settings, then Tutorial and help.

Remember to turn off TalkBack when you are done: hold both volume keys.

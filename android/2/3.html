Part 2, step 3 of 5 Links and buttons

TalkBack says “link” or “button” after the name. A link takes you somewhere else. A button does something on the page you are on. That is why it matters which one we use.

You can jump between links with the reading controls. Swipe up then down in one movement to change the reading control. On phones with multi-finger gestures you can also swipe up or down with three fingers. TalkBack says the name of each control. Keep going until you hear “Links”. Then swipe down to go to the next one, and up to go to the previous one.

Try it on the news below. Jump between the links and listen. Which link do you understand without hearing the text around it?

Three links are called “Read more”. When you jump between links, you don't know where they go. The last link says what it is about.

The Favourite button tells you whether it is on or off. Double-tap it and listen to what TalkBack says.

Swiping left and right still moves one thing at a time, whatever the reading controls is set to. Swipe right until you hear “Next”, and double-tap.

Part 2, step 1 of 5 Headings

Most screen reader users don't read a page from the top. They jump between headings to get an overview, just as you skim with your eyes. Two out of three start this way on a long page.

You jump between headings with the reading controls. Swipe up then down in one movement to change the reading control. On phones with multi-finger gestures you can also swipe up or down with three fingers. TalkBack says the name of each control. Keep going until you hear “Headings”. Then swipe down to go to the next one, and up to go to the previous one.

If you never hear “Headings”, add it: open the TalkBack menu with three fingers, choose TalkBack settings, then Edit menus, then Customize reading controls, and turn on Headings.

TalkBack also says the heading level, such as “heading level 3”. The levels tell you how the page is organised.

Try it: find out when Fjordby Library closes on Saturday. Use headings to jump there.

Part 2, step 2 of 5 Landmarks

Landmarks are the main areas of a page: the header at the top, navigation menus, the main content and the footer. Some screen reader users jump between them to skip things that repeat on every page, such as menus.

Landmarks are in the reading controls too. Swipe up then down in one movement to change the reading control. On phones with multi-finger gestures you can also swipe up or down with three fingers. TalkBack says the name of each control. Keep going until you hear “Landmarks”. Then swipe down to go to the next one, and up to go to the previous one.

If you never hear “Landmarks”, add it: open the TalkBack menu with three fingers, choose TalkBack settings, then Edit menus, then Customize reading controls, and turn on Landmarks. If your version of TalkBack has no Landmarks option, read this step and move on.

Try it: this page has a header, the main content with a practice area, the menu at the bottom called “Steps”, and a footer. Jump to the menu called “Steps”, then back to the main content.

Each landmark needs a name when there is more than one of the same kind. That is why the menu at the bottom is called “Steps”, so you know what kind of navigation it is.
